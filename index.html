About

Ajay Prince

I've spent 20 years building trading, risk and investment systems at BNP Paribas, Man Group and Citi — including a VP role on Citi Velocity — and I now work on AI-enabled financial products at Virtuetech. I founded Apptivate Ltd in 2015 to give growing teams access to that same senior engineering judgement without hiring a full-time CTO in London.

I'm based in London and work full-stack across React and TypeScript, .NET and C#, Node, and hands-on AI and LLM integration. Delivery is led from the UK and built with a trusted team in Chennai, so you get senior direction and a cost-effective build.
