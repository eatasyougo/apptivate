Cookie notice

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This notice explains how Apptivate Limited uses cookies and similar technologies on apptivate.uk. It should be read with our privacy notice.

What we use

This website is a static site. We do not set advertising cookies, we do not run a cookie banner for tracking, and we do not use Google Analytics or similar tools on these pages.

Fonts are hosted with the site, so your browser does not fetch them from a third-party font service.

Our host (GitHub Pages) may process technical logs and set cookies that are strictly necessary to deliver the site securely. We do not control those server logs.

Third-party services you choose to use

If you book a call, you leave this site and use Calendly. Calendly sets its own cookies and processes your details under its privacy notice.

If you send the contact form, the request goes to Formspree. Formspree may set cookies on its own domain and processes the message under the privacy terms published on formspree.io.

Those cookies are not set by us on apptivate.uk. We recommend you only use those services if you are comfortable with their notices.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking all cookies may stop some third-party booking or form pages from working. Because we do not use non-essential cookies on this site, UK PECR does not require a consent banner here.
