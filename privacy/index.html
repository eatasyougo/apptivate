Privacy notice

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This notice explains how Apptivate Limited (“Apptivate”, “we”, “us”) handles personal data when you use apptivate.uk, email us, send the contact form, or book a call. It is written for a small UK company. It is not legal advice.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Apptivate Limited, company number 09654478, registered in England and Wales. Registered office: 20 Allenswood Road, London SE9 6RP.

Contact: info@apptivate.uk

What we collect

We only collect what we need to reply and, if we work together, to deliver the work:

  • Contact form: name, email, company (if you give it), message, and the consent tick. The form is processed by Formspree.
  • Email: whatever you send to info@apptivate.uk, including any attachments.
  • Booking: if you use Calendly, they collect your name, email and meeting details so we can hold the call. That happens on Calendly’s site under their terms.
  • Hosting logs: this site is a static site on GitHub Pages. The host may log technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and to serve the site.

We do not run advertising or analytics tags on this website. We do not buy marketing lists or sell your details.

Why we use it

  • To answer an enquiry or book a call — legitimate interests, and consent where you tick the form box (UK GDPR Articles 6(1)(f) and 6(1)(a)).
  • To provide services, invoicing and support if you become a client — contract (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep records we are required to keep — legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).

Who we share it with

We share personal data only as needed to run the business. Typical recipients:

  • Formspree, for the contact form
  • Calendly, if you book a call through their link
  • GitHub, which hosts this website
  • Our email and office providers
  • Our delivery team in Chennai, India, where we need them to quote or do the work, acting on our instructions
  • Professional advisers, insurers, or authorities if the law requires it

Some of those organisations are outside the UK. Where that happens, we rely on an adequacy decision or other safeguards they publish (for example standard contractual clauses).

How long we keep it

Enquiry and booking details are kept only as long as we need them to respond and follow up — usually up to 24 months if we do not start work, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. If you become a client, we keep records for the life of the engagement and for a reasonable period afterwards (typically up to six years) for tax, accounting and legal reasons.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • correct it if it is wrong
  • erase it, or restrict or object to how we use it
  • receive a copy in a portable format, where that right applies
  • withdraw consent, where we rely on consent — that does not affect use before you withdraw

Email info@apptivate.uk. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Security

We use reputable providers and restrict access to people who need it. No website or email system is completely secure; please do not send passwords or payment card numbers through the contact form.

Cookies

See our cookie notice. This site does not use non-essential cookies.

Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top is the latest version. If we make a material change, we will update this page.
